B2B · Investor Outreach & Appointment Setting

Red Panda Leads

190+appointments in one month

The client sells an AI education program to investors. His 5 in-house setters were booking about 1 appointment a day each, and the business was closing 2 deals a month. After two months, we took over the whole operation with the same 5 agents.

  • 190+ appointments booked in our first full month
  • 10+ closed deals that month, up from 2
  • Same 5 agents, now run on our playbook and QA
Real Estate Wholesaling · Outbound Acquisition

TrueParity

1 → 10dedicated callers in 6 months

A U.S. wholesaling company that had never outsourced before. We built and ran their outbound acquisition team, scaling from 1 seat to 10 dedicated callers in 6 months, with KPI-based supervision, daily QA and CRM-ready leads.

  • 154K+ dials a month
  • 400+ qualified seller leads a month
  • About 40 qualified leads per caller, every month
Automotive · Multilingual Claims Acquisition

Autobody Werks

129+insurance claims in 6 months

A U.S. auto body shop needed insurance claims from a multilingual market. When their lead specialist left, we planned two months ahead and rebuilt the role as a 3-agent Arabic, Chinese and English team.

  • Beat the previous single-agent peak in month one
  • More than doubled it by month two
  • 129+ claims secured in 6 months
B2B · Chinese-Language Sales Channel

Order Meal

10restaurant partners onboarded

A New Zealand meal ordering platform wanted to reach Chinese restaurants. We translated scripts, contracts and client communication into Chinese, then recruited and managed a Chinese-speaking outbound team.

  • 10 partners onboarded against a 1-per-month target
  • Full sales channel built from scratch
  • Workflows, QA and reporting in place
