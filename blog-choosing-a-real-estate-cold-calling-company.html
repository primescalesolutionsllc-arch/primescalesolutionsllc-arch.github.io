5 questions to ask before you hire a real estate cold calling company

Not all calling companies are the same. These questions separate the good ones from the rest.

Before you sign with any calling company, ask these five questions. The answers will tell you a lot.

1. What exactly is a qualified lead? If they cannot define it clearly, you will argue about it every month.

2. Can I hear real calls? Any good company will share real recordings. If they only show scripts, be careful.

3. Who manages my callers day to day? You want a named person who reviews calls daily and knows your numbers.

4. What happens when a caller quits? Replacement time matters. Weeks of empty seats kill momentum.

5. What do you guarantee, and what happens if you miss it? A company that believes in its process will put something on the line.
