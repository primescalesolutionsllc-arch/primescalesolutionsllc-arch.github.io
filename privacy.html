Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Prime Scale Solutions LLC ("Prime Scale", "we", "us") provides managed outbound calling and remote team services. You can contact us at primescalesolutionsllc@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you contact us or apply for a job, we collect the information you choose to give us, such as your name, company, email, phone number, state, the service you are interested in, your message and any CV you send. Like most websites, our hosting provider may also log basic technical data such as your browser type and IP address.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to your inquiry, provide and improve our services, review job applications, and send you service-related communications. We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number and agree to be contacted, we may text you about your inquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Mobile numbers and SMS consent are never shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Sharing

We only share information with service providers who help us run our business (for example email, hosting and CRM tools), and only as needed to provide our services, or when required by law.

Client data

When we work on a client campaign, we handle the client's data only to deliver the agreed services, following the client's instructions and reasonable security practices.

Retention

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing primescalesolutionsllc@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
